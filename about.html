Why Rediscovering YOU

Who you are beneath the roles

Laura created Rediscovering YOU for capable women who have spent years taking care of everything and everyone — and quietly lost connection with their own needs, identity, intuition, and sense of direction. The thread is simple: reconnect with who you are beneath responsibilities, expectations, old patterns, and life roles. Lives that look good on paper still deserve a self that feels alive inside them.

Background

Nearly two decades in schools

As a school psychologist, Laura has walked alongside children, families, and educators through growth, stress, and change. That depth in human development, behavior, and nervous-system regulation informs how she coaches adults today — with the steadiness of clinical training, without asking you to become a patient.

How she works

Psychology-informed coaching

Psychology-informed coaching that integrates nervous-system awareness, intuitive work, and practical change. A room where you can be honest, and leave with something you can use. Podcast host. Reiki Master. Retreat facilitator. One person behind the work.

Coaching is not therapy. This work is informed by psychology, behavior, nervous-system regulation, and human development. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace mental health care. If you need clinical support, Laura will say so clearly and kindly.
