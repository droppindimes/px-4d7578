Why Rediscovering YOU

I created this for women who have spent years taking care of everything and everyone — and quietly lost connection with themselves. Reconnect with who you are beneath the roles. Lives that look pretty good on paper can still leave you asking: is this really it?

Nearly two decades in schools

As a school psychologist, I’ve walked alongside children, families, and educators through growth, stress, and change. Twenty years with kids, families, and schools is how I sit with adults now.

How I work

A room where you can be honest — and leave with something you can actually use. Reiki if it fits.

Coaching is not therapy. This work is informed by psychology, behavior, nervous-system regulation, and human development. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace mental health care. If you need clinical support, I will say so clearly and kindly.
