Background

Nearly two decades in schools

As a school psychologist, Laura has walked alongside children, families, and educators through growth, stress, and change. That depth in human development, behavior, and nervous-system regulation informs how she coaches adults today — with the steadiness of clinical training, without turning the work into a clinical office.

Why Rediscovering YOU

Who you are beneath the roles

Rediscovering YOU is the thread that connects coaching, podcast, Reiki, and future retreats: helping capable women reconnect with who they are beneath responsibilities, expectations, old patterns, and life roles. Lives that look good on paper still deserve a self that feels alive inside them.

How she works

Psychology-informed coaching

Psychology-informed coaching that integrates nervous-system awareness, intuitive work, and practical change. Credible and compassionate — a room where you can be honest, and leave with something you can use.

Coaching is not therapy. This work is informed by psychology, behavior, nervous-system regulation, and human development. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace mental health care. If you need clinical support, Laura will say so clearly and kindly.
