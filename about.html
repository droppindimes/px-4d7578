The story

Who I am

I’ve spent nearly two decades working in psychology and supporting people. Rediscovering YOU grew out of something broader than my professional background.

I know what it’s like to build a full life, take care of everyone and everything, check the boxes, and still reach points where you have to ask yourself, “Wait — where am I in all of this?”

Why Rediscovering YOU

Different seasons of life, relationships, motherhood, career, loss, change, growth and simply getting older can all shift who we are. Sometimes the version of us that worked for one season doesn’t fit the next one.

That’s really the heart of Rediscovering YOU for me.

How I work

The blend I bring to this work is psychology, nervous system awareness, coaching, intuition, energy work/Reiki, and real-life experience. I don’t see those as competing approaches.

I think there’s value in understanding why we think and behave the way we do while also paying attention to what we feel, what our bodies are telling us, and what we actually want.

This isn’t therapy, and I won’t pretend it is.

I’m a school psychologist, a coach, a Reiki Master, a podcast host, and a retreat facilitator. Those roles support this work. They aren’t the story.

If you want to hear where I am right now, start with the podcast — What I’m Rediscovering Right Now.

Coaching is not therapy. This work is informed by psychology, behavior, nervous-system regulation, and human development. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace mental health care. If you need clinical support, I will say so clearly and kindly.
