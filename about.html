Why Rediscovering YOU

I created this space for women who have spent years taking care of everything and everyone — and quietly lost connection with themselves along the way. A life can look good on paper and still leave you asking: is this really it? The work here is reconnecting with who you are beneath the roles.

Nearly two decades in schools

As a school psychologist, I’ve spent nearly twenty years walking alongside children, families, and educators through growth, stress, and change. That same steady presence is how I sit with adults now.

How I work

A space where you can be honest — and leave with something you can actually use. Reiki, if it fits.

Coaching is not therapy. This work is informed by psychology, behavior, nervous-system regulation, and human development. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace mental health care. If you need clinical support, I’ll say so clearly and kindly.
